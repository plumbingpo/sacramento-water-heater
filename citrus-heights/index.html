Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Citrus Heights

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush every year if their water is hard.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, rumbling, or moisture under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater after a leak.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, ignored for a few years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and shorten the life of the tank, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Hot Water Headaches Citrus Heights Homeowners Face

Every type of water heater fails in its own way, and we work on all of them in Citrus Heights. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
