Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
